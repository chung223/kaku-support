Kaku 隱私權政策

最後更新：2026-09-27

Kaku 不蒐集你的任何資料。

照片與影片

拍好後存到你的「照片」App，最近 60 張也留在 Kaku 自己的資料夾，方便在 App 裡看。底片機模式拍的照片，沖洗前只存在 Kaku 的資料夾。刪除照片時，Kaku 會請你同意完整的相簿權限，只用來刪除你選的那張。

位置

只在你點「看今天的魔幻時刻」時使用大概位置，用來算日落與藍調時刻；座標只留在手機上，不會上傳。

相機、麥克風、動作感測

只在拍攝時使用。構圖分析（人物姿勢、場景分類）和拍後講評的文字，都在手機上計算，不會上傳。

Apple Watch

手錶上的取景畫面直接從 iPhone 傳到你的手錶，不經過網路。

購買

Kaku Pro 透過 App Store 付款。Kaku 看不到你的付款資料，只知道有沒有解鎖。

其他

Kaku 沒有帳號、沒有廣告、沒有分析或追蹤工具，也不會把資料分享給任何人。

Kaku Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Kaku does not collect any of your data.

Photos and videos

They are saved to your Photos app, and the last 60 are also kept in Kaku’s own folder so you can view them in the app. Film mode shots stay only in Kaku’s folder until they’re developed. When you delete a photo, Kaku asks for full photo library access and uses it only to delete that photo.

Location

Used only when you tap “See today’s golden hour”, and only approximately, to work out sunset and blue hour. It stays on your iPhone and is never uploaded.

Camera, microphone, and motion

Used only while shooting. Composition analysis (poses, scene types) and shot feedback are computed on your iPhone and never uploaded.

Apple Watch

The viewfinder goes straight from your iPhone to your watch, not over the internet.

Purchases

Kaku Pro is paid through the App Store. Kaku never sees your payment details, only whether Pro is unlocked.

Everything else

Kaku has no accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking, and shares nothing with anyone.
