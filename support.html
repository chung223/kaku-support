Kaku 支援

有問題或建議，請寫信到 kaku@chung.men，附上 iPhone 機型與 iOS 版本會比較快找到原因。

常見問題

換手機或重新安裝後，Kaku Pro 不見了？
打開 Kaku 的設定（右上角齒輪）或付費畫面，點「回復購買」。要用當初購買的 Apple 帳號。

怎麼取消訂閱？
在 iPhone 的「設定」>「Apple 帳號」>「訂閱項目」取消。訂閱結束後 Pro 功能會停用，拍過的照片都會留著。

為什麼刪除照片時要完整的相簿權限？
Kaku 平常只要求「加入照片」的權限。刪除時需要完整權限，才能把「照片」App 裡的同一張一起刪掉；Kaku 不會讀其他照片。

Apple Watch 遙控連不上？
先在 iPhone 上打開 Kaku，讓它停在相機畫面；手錶遙控需要 Kaku 在前景。

從鎖定畫面打開 Kaku？
把「Kaku 相機」控制項加到鎖定畫面或控制中心，或在「設定」>「相機」>「相機控制」指定給相機控制按鈕。

Questions or suggestions? Email kaku@chung.men. Including your iPhone model and iOS version helps.

FAQ

Kaku Pro disappeared after switching phones or reinstalling.
Open Kaku’s Settings (the gear icon) or the paywall and tap Restore Purchases, using the Apple Account you bought with.

How do I cancel my subscription?
In the iPhone Settings app, go to Apple Account > Subscriptions. When a subscription ends, Pro features turn off, but your photos stay.

Why does deleting a photo ask for full photo library access?
Kaku normally asks only to add photos. Deleting needs full access so the same photo can be removed from the Photos app; Kaku doesn’t read your other photos.

The Apple Watch remote won’t connect.
Open Kaku on your iPhone and leave it on the camera screen. The remote needs Kaku in the foreground.

How do I open Kaku from the Lock Screen?
Add the Kaku Camera control to the Lock Screen or Control Center, or assign it to Camera Control in Settings > Camera > Camera Control.
